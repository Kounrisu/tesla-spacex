# Tesla and SpaceX

A public learning notebook about the products, engineering, science, and major developments around Tesla and SpaceX.

## Topics

- **Tesla** — vehicles, batteries, charging, manufacturing, energy products, software, and product announcements.
- **SpaceX** — launch vehicles, spacecraft, launch operations, reusability, Starlink, and program milestones.
- **Rocket science** — forces and motion, orbital mechanics, propulsion, staging, heat, guidance, and recovery.
- **News and releases** — dated summaries with links to original announcements and independent reporting.

## How to study a claim

Record the date and source. Prefer technical documentation, filings, mission updates, and other primary material for what the companies say; use independent reporting and technical references to check context. Label estimates and company targets clearly, and distinguish plans from demonstrated results.

## Learning sequence

1. Build a glossary of vehicle, spacecraft, battery, and launch terms.
2. Learn the basic physics of reaching orbit and returning a booster.
3. Map each product or vehicle to its purpose, major subsystems, and trade-offs.
4. Keep a dated timeline of important tests, launches, releases, and product changes.
5. Explain each topic in plain language, then link the sources used.
